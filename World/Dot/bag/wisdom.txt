Me And A Tree - poem
Trees shed their leaves to bear new ones.
I focus on one thing at a time and leave others for some time
because I am up for something new.
A tree takes and uses energy very optimally. So shall I.
I shall bear new branches
as the tree does for its growth.
The trees do not nood no exercise. But I do.
As the season changes, the tree bears its fruits
so shall my education at times give me
what I NEED. Just as the tree continuously
seeks for water, so shall I seek fo what I DESIRE.

   Plant a tree. See it grow. Count
   the leaves and flowers. Soon
   there will be more to count than you can 
   and realise - the time spent 
   was put on growing the tree
   - every moment.
